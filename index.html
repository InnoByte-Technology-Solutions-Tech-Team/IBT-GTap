The problem

What replacing a spreadsheet actually fixes

Manual tools don't fail loudly. They fail quietly, one broken formula or missing entry at a time — until the data you need isn't there.

The spreadsheet breaks

One wrong formula and last week's attendance is gone. No one can say which copy is current, or who last edited it.

Paper sign-in sheets vanish

A logbook at the front desk doesn't hold up when you need to know who actually renewed, or prove it later.

No real-time visibility

You find out a membership expired when the member is already at the desk — not before, when you could act on it.

Platform

Every module a growing gym needs

Built for owners and multi-branch operators — one console, without the enterprise bloat you'd get elsewhere.

Check-in

NFC / RFID tap check-in

Members tap a card or phone at the desk. Status, timestamp, and visit history are logged automatically — no manual entry.

Billing

Automated membership billing

Charge members through PayMongo. Memberships extend the moment payment clears, with no follow-up needed from staff.

Multi-branch

Run every location from one account

Owners see all branches from a single login, with reporting that breaks down cleanly by location.

Scheduling

Classes and locker management

Schedule classes, track bookings, and assign lockers without a second spreadsheet running alongside the first.

Analytics

Attendance and retention data

See peak hours, retention rate, and daily traffic across every branch, updated as check-ins happen.

Reliability

Works when the internet doesn't

Front desk keeps checking members in if the connection drops. Everything syncs automatically once it's back.

Trust & reliability

Built to standards a gym owner can rely on

The parts you don't see are held to the same bar as the parts you do.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Member and payment data is encrypted end to end, on every device and every branch.

Role-based staff access

Owners control exactly what front-desk staff can see and do — no shared logins required.

PCI-DSS compliant billing

Offline-first architecture
